Proportional Reasoning Challenge Lab

Unit rates · proportionality · equations · graphs · multistep reasoning

Reference: reasoning tools

Unit rate: divide to find the amount for one unit. Include units in your interpretation.

Proportionality: y/x must be constant for every pair with x ≠ 0, and a proportional graph passes through (0, 0).

Equation: y = kx, where k is the constant of proportionality.

Fractions: divide by a fraction by multiplying by its reciprocal. Keep exact values until the final step.

Percent change: new amount = original amount × (1 ± rate as a decimal).

Think carefully. Explain relationships, not just arithmetic.
