Reference guide: rates, ratios, and proportions
  • Unit rate: divide to find the amount for 1 unit (such as dollars per pound).
  • Constant of proportionality: in y = kx, find k = y ÷ x.
  • Proportional table: the ratio y ÷ x stays the same in every row.
  • Proportional graph: a straight line through the origin (0, 0).
  • Scale up or down: multiply or divide both quantities by the same number.
  • Answer with units when describing a rate. Round only when the question asks you to.
